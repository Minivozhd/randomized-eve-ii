PE1 music mutator — bundled music pack, PE2 USA, version 1.3.1

Enable Advanced Options > Parasite Eve 1 music. The ordinary music checkbox
and its configuration button are disabled while this mutator is active.
Disabling the mutator restores the previous music randomization setting.
Old presets keep their original meaning; new enabled presets use a PEM1 trailer.

The mutator boosts the 37 replacement song levels by approximately 30%
(+2.3 dB), preserving their channel mix and native master/fade controls.
Restart the rebuilt app and regenerate disc images to apply the louder mix.

The user-selected 37 tracks/phases are assigned to PEACEFUL (7), EXPLORING (9),
INTENSE (7), and FIGHT (14). Source IDs 0x18 and 0x1D are excluded. MISC scenes
use the combined PE1 pool. Existing per-scene forbidden PE2 IDs remain respected.

pe1-music.pack is an XOR patch against verified PE2 USA music bank bytes.
It is included in every release package and in the source repository.
The app expects assets/game/music/pe1-music.pack below its resource root.
Keep the full release package together; no separate download is needed.
Missing, damaged, or mismatched packs fail before generation; all original bank
preimages are checked before the first bank is written. CDF and HED extents do
not grow. Both original disc images remain read-only.

These are adapted native hMPK/MIDI banks, not MP3 playback or emulator hooks.
The original PS1 RAM and MIDI capacities are retained. Repeated phrases use
native F5/F6 commands; loops use native NRPN commands. Original instrument
ADSR is retained. Large banks have explicitly recorded half-rate samples.
Songs exceeding channel/sequence limits lose quieter source parts and use
coarser automation.
Short loops are reconstructed where two backward jumps were captured; other
parts repeat the extracted phrase, so a source intro may repeat. Reverb uses
PE2's existing preset 3 with PE1 sends/depth, not the preview's PE1 preset 4.
Signed stereo gains are folded to magnitude. This is not bit-exact PE1 playback.

Native CPU tests mock physical SPU allocation and DMA. In-game listening,
transitions, battle/SFX coexistence, fades, CD timing and original PlayStation
hardware validation remain separate pending checks. Donor conversion and native verification research stay outside this source
repository. Publication of the bundled patch was approved for v1.3.1.

Track 0x26 has a stronger arrangement adaptation: one quiet melodic lane is
omitted; eight accompaniment lanes share a tuned key-map, with fixed median
gain/pan. Their original first 32.94 seconds are followed by a repeating
4.71-second passage. The main melody retains its complete extracted phrase.
Native stream capacity remains 18 tracks and 16 channels; physical voice
allocation and release tails still require listening in the game.
